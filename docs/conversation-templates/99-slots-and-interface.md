# The mod's own words — slots, world words and interface text

> These live in `assets/mcaconversations/lang/`, not in `mca_dialogue`. They have no personality overlays: one wording serves every villager.

> Read [README.md](README.md) first.

## `mcaconversations.slot.`

Noun phrases the scene generator drops into a generated line as %2$s, %3$s… — "a rotten post", "a gate that sticks". They must read naturally mid-sentence, lower case, no full stop.
